## 3. Project Theme Discussion
AI Relationship & Dependency Dashboard
What it does:
- AI-assisted skill degradation monitor  
  Tracks and prevents skill loss (e.g., writing, mental math, memory, researching) as AI automates tasks. Aims to mitigate societal risks of over-reliance on AI.
- AI-integrated digital well-being dashboard  
  Tracks and manages a user's AI usage patterns (across platforms, if feasible). As AI becomes part of daily life, people need insight into how they live with and rely on AI.
- Explain-My-AI Transparency Tool  
  Desktop utility that helps users understand AI decision-making, promoting responsible use of AI.
  Features:
  Usage Tracking
- Detects when AI tools are used
- Categorises usage: writing, research, coding, planning, automation, friend/confidant
  Dependency Analysis
- Detects over-reliance patterns
- Example insights:
    - "You used AI to summarize 7 news articles and 2 research papers this week."
    - "Math problems solved by AI increased 15% this week."
      Skill Preservation
- Tailored mini-challenges based on skill degradation
- "AI-free writing day" with a prompt
- Memory or reasoning exercises
  Explain-My-AI
- Shows simplified explanation of AI decision-making process
- Confidence indicators
- Bias indicators    
  Healthy AI Suggestions
- "Try solving this yourself first."
- "Use AI for brainstorming instead of writing the whole text."
  Possible Names:
- CogniAware – Know how AI shapes your thinking
- MindfulAI – Use AI consciously
- AIBalance – Keeping humans in the loop
