**ER:** AI Relationship & Dependency Dashboard
What it does:
- AI-assisted skill degradation monitor (Combine with DS's idea)
  Description: As AI tools automate more tasks, humans will inevitably lose skills (e.g., writing, mental math, memory, researching). This app tracks and prevents that with the goal of addressing the potential societal risk that over-reliance on AI could present.
- AI-integrated digital well-being dashboard  
  Description: Tracks and manages a user's AI usage patterns (across platforms if feasible). As AI becomes part of daily life, people need insight into how they live with and rely on AI. This would be through the questionnaire suggested by the tutor at first unless we can develop something more interactive (i.e. AI chatbot in the app?). Example questionnaire (but for businesses) https://pathfinder.insentra.ai/assessment
- Explain-My-AI Transparency Tool  
  Description: Desktop utility that helps users understand how AI decisions are made. It supports responsible AI use in a world where AI is everywhere.
  Features
  Usage Tracking
- Detects when AI tools are used (No longer feasible unless we use a chatbot)
- Categorizes usage (writing, research, coding, planning, automation, friend/confidant).
  Dependency Analysis
- Detects over-reliance patterns.
- Example insights:
    - “You used AI to summarize 7 news articles and 2 research papers this week.”
    - “Math problems solved by AI increased 15% this week.”
      Skill Preservation
- Tailored based on skill degradation.
- Mini challenges.
- “AI-free writing day” with a prompt.
- Memory or reasoning exercises.
  Explain-My-AI
- Shows simplified explanations of an AI's decision-making process.
- Confidence indicators.
- Bias indicators.
  Healthy AI Suggestions
- “Try solving this yourself first.”
- “Use AI for brainstorming instead of writing the whole text.”
  Possible Names
- CogniAware — Know how AI shapes your thinking.
- MindfulAI — Use AI consciously.
- AIBalance — Keeping humans in the loop.
