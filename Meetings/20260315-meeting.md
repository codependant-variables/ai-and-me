# 4. Project Requirements Discussion

## 4.1 Feature Ideas

### Skill Atrophy Measurement

A system to track how reliance on AI affects skill retention.

Proposed approach:

1. Users complete a quiz in a chosen subject area.
2. The quiz can be pre-made or AI-generated.
3. Answers are automatically marked.
4. Users repeat the quiz after a set period.
5. The system compares results to measure skill retention or degradation.
6. Results are displayed on the user dashboard.

---

### AI News Aggregation

A dashboard section displaying AI-related news.

Possible features:

* Article headlines
* Article thumbnails
* Source name and icon
* List of articles
* Clicking an article opens the full story in the browser

Potential APIs:

* [https://newsapi.org/docs/client-libraries/java](https://newsapi.org/docs/client-libraries/java)
* [https://newsdata.io/](https://newsdata.io/)
* [https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis](https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis)

---

### Environmental Impact Estimation

Estimate the environmental impact of AI usage.

Concept:

* Calculate approximate energy and water consumption from AI usage.
* Base calculations on research data and estimated usage.

---

### Account System

The application will include a user authentication system.

Features:

* User registration
* Secure login
* Persistent database
* Data storage across sessions

---

### AI Usage & Dependency Dashboard

A dashboard designed to help users understand and manage their AI usage.

Potential components:

**AI Usage Tracking**

* Categorise AI usage (writing, research, coding, planning)

**Dependency Analysis**

* Identify patterns of over-reliance on AI.

Example insights:

* “You used AI to summarise 7 articles this week.”
* “Math problems solved by AI increased 15% this week.”

**Skill Preservation**

* Suggest challenges or activities to reduce reliance on AI.
* Examples:

  * AI-free writing prompts
  * Memory exercises
  * Reasoning tasks
