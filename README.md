# AI&Me

> Encouraging mindful and balanced AI usage through self-reflection, quizzes, and habit tracking.

## Introduction

AI&Me is an application that helps users reflect on how AI impacts their thinking, learning, and problem-solving habits. The app promotes balanced AI usage through tracking, quizzes, and self-assessment tools.

---

## Problem Statement

As AI tools become more common, users may become overly dependent on them for cognitive and creative tasks, leading to reduced independent problem-solving and skill retention.

AI&Me aims to:

* Track AI usage habits
* Encourage critical thinking
* Support skill retention
* Promote intentional AI engagement

Reference:
[Harvard Gazette – Is AI dulling our minds?](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2025/11/is-ai-dulling-our-minds/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## Key Features

* User Authentication (Sign Up / Log In)
* User Profiles & Settings
* Dark Theme Support
* Dashboard with AI usage insights
* Check-In System
* AI Happiness & Dependence Tracking
* Quiz System with:

    * Moral Scenarios
    * Mental Maths
    * Reading Comprehension
    * Word Problems
    * Puzzles
* Puzzle-based quiz mode
* Multiple-choice quiz support
* Randomized quiz generation

---

## Conception Ideas

AI&Me was created to encourage healthier and more intentional AI usage. Rather than discouraging AI entirely, the app helps users stay aware of how AI affects their learning, habits, and critical thinking skills.
